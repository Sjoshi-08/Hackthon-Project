#Spotly
Spotly is a location-based platform that help users discover nearby places, offers and activities happening in their area.

##About the project
-Spotly connects local users with business and event organizer in their area.
-Local business owner can promote their events and offer to people nearby. 

##Features
-Explore nearby places
-Discover new Events
-Get updated about upcoming local events
-Promote upcoming events for your area.
-Send updates and notification to users.

##Technologies used
-HTML
-CSS
-JAVASCRIPT
-BACKEND TECHNOLOGIES

##Goal
The goal of Spotly is to make local discovery easier by connecting people
with nearby businesses, events, offers, and activities while helping local businesses reach their potential customers.
